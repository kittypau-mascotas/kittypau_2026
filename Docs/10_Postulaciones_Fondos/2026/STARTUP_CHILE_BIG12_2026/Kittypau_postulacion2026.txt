Start-Up Chile Programs



Build - Light on!

Our 4-month pre-acceleration program is designed for early-stage, tech-based startups with less than one year of commercial development and/or less than one year of initiation of activities in Chile's Servicio de Impuestos Internos (SII). Selected projects will receive CLP $15 million (approximately USD $16,000) in equity-free funding, along with soft-landing support and a hands-on pre-acceleration program. Startups can apply for an extension of the program, and the top performers will advance directly to our next funding initiative, Ignite. This program has two application calls per year, each selecting around 15-25 startups.



Ignite - Speed up!

Our 4-month acceleration program for tech-based startups with at least a functional minimum viable product (MVP) or a functional prototype. Eligible startups must have less than 3 years of commercial development and/or less than 3 years of initiation of activities in Chile's Servicio de Impuestos Internos (SII). Selected projects will receive CLP $30 million (approximately USD $33,000) in equity-free funding, along with soft-landing support and a hands-on acceleration program. Startups can apply for an extension of the program, and the top performers will receive an additional CLP $20 million, totaling CLP $50 million in equity-free funding. This program has two application calls per year, each selecting around 25-40 startups.



Growth - Take off!

Our 6-month acceleration program for scaling stage tech-based startups, with accumulated sales over the last 12 months between 2.400 UF and 25.000 UF. The program seeks the best technological founders in the expansion stage of their businesses to elevate their high-impact startups to the next level. Selected companies receive CLP $75 million (approximately USD $82,000) of equity-free funding, soft-landing support, access to global mentors, connections with investment funds, and contact with national and international networks for business expansion. This program has two application calls per year, selecting around 10-20 startups per call.

For the Ignite and Growth programs, you may register up to 3 Executors.
This information cannot be modified once the application has been submitted.
